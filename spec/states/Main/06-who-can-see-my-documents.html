Questions

What people ask us first.

Why not just use Claude or ChatGPT?

You can, and our method runs in Claude. What we add is the structure: a specialist agent for each area, a second AI checking each finding, every claim graded and sourced, and a memo your committee can read. The platform adds a workflow your team shares across a deal or a whole portfolio, and a secure vault for your evidence and memos.

If the method is free, why pay for the platform?

The method tells you what to do. The platform does it for you: it runs the agents at the same time, has a second AI check their work, keeps your evidence in one place, updates the memo when new data arrives, and lets your team approve each step. If you would rather run the method in your own AI assistant, go ahead: that is why it is free.

Can I use my Claude subscription?

For the free method, yes: install it in Claude and run it within your plan. The platform connects to your AI provider's developer account instead, and the provider bills its usage to you directly.

What does the AI usage cost?

A small part of the total. You see an estimate before anything runs, and you set a cap that the run will not cross.

Who can see my documents?

Only your team. Agents work in isolation, never hold your keys, and cannot send anything out. The security page has the detail.

When should I hire you instead?

When the deal is large, when you need customer interviews, or when your committee wants an operator's view. You can start on the platform and bring us in for the deals that matter most.
