The main trunk of the method has five stages, from the market to the first 100 days. Supporting skills feed them with cohorts, interviews and a user panel.

Stage 1, Market

How big is the market?

A first-principles view of the market, then a sourced deep dive: sizing, value chain, players, M&A and the five trends that matter.
