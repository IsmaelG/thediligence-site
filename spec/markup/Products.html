01 · Plan

Know the cost before you build the memo.

No run starts without a plan you have seen. The coordinator drafts it in seconds: the sequence of agents, what each one will cost, what it still needs from you, and where it will stop for your approval.

  • Every agent has a price. Estimated from the agent’s own profile and the list prices of the providers you connected.
  • Change a model, watch the total move. Pick Economy, Balanced or Frontier, or set a tier agent by agent.
  • A cap the run obeys. If a run would cross your budget, it stops and delivers what exists, with a list of what is missing.
  • Estimate against actual. After the run, each agent shows its drift, and its profile updates itself.

04 · Evidence and lineage

New evidence rewrites only what it touches.

Every artifact records what it was built from. When a file arrives, the platform computes the sections that went stale, shows why, and prices the rebuild before you approve it.

  • One drop zone. Files, links and pasted text are stored, read, classified, graded and tagged to the sections they touch.
  • Three data classes. Public, target-confidential and fund-internal. The plan shows which provider will read which class, before launch.
  • Ask the founder. Turn an item in the appendix into a request with an upload link that needs no account. You approve what lands.
  • Interviews move the grades most. Loyal and churned customers, competitor founders and teams: each call becomes a source and upgrades the claims it touches. Upload it, or book time with us.
  • Evidence is untrusted. A deck can contain instructions. Agents treat it as data, and have no tool that sends anything out.

05 · Vault At launch

A vault we host, and cannot read.

Term sheets, partner notes and your own view of a founder belong in a vault, not in a prompt. It is hosted by us and encrypted in your browser. The agents read nothing until you say so.

  • Hosted, encrypted in your browser. The key comes from your passphrase and stays with you. We store only ciphertext. Lose the passphrase and the recovery code, and the vault is gone: we say so plainly.
  • Agents are locked out by default. A note reaches a run only when you share it for that target. The read is logged.
  • Plain markdown, no lock-in. Notes with links between them. Import an Obsidian vault, export a zip that Obsidian opens unchanged.
  • Linked to the work. A note can link a target, a source or a section, and shows up as a backlink in its lineage.
  • Prefer Obsidian? From Pro. At launch A plugin in your vault pairs with a one-time code and shares only the notes you mark. The rest stays on your side, and we hold no key to it.

Another vault? Suggest it. The requests we count decide what we connect next.

06 · Workflow

Agents and people, one workflow.

A diligence is not done by agents alone, nor by people alone. TheDiligence is the place where they work on the same file. Agents plan, read and draft. People decide, challenge and bring what no database holds. Each reaches the other where it already works: by email, in the app, and through MCP.

  1. Agent

    The coordinator drafts the plan and its price.

    In the app
  2. You

    You approve the plan, or edit the steering note to tell it what to weigh.

    App or email
  3. Agents

    Specialist agents run, in parallel wherever the method allows, each in its own sandbox.

    In the app
  4. Agent

    A gate opens before the verdict, with what was found and what is still doubtful.

    Email
  5. You

    You answer the gate, and add what only people can get: an interview, an export, a view.

    App or email
  6. Team

    Partners comment on a section. A comment can become a steering note for the agents.

    In the app
  7. Agents

    Only the sections touched by the new evidence are rebuilt, and priced before you approve.

    In the app
  8. Your assistant Next

    Your own agents and personal assistants take part through MCP (Next): start a plan, read a memo, add evidence or answer a gate.

    MCP

Home is an inbox, and so is your email

What needs you, what is running, what was delivered, across every target. Answer a gate with one click, in the app or from the notification email.

Open to your own agents

An MCP interface (Next) lets the agents and assistants you already use take part in the work. They propose and fetch. A person still opens every gate.

Requests that close the loop

The data still needed becomes an email draft and an upload link. Statuses: requested, received, ingested. A delta run follows.

Changes since you last looked

The verdict held or moved, which sections were rewritten and because of which evidence, what it cost. Mark it reviewed.

Many targets, one workspace

A fund or an advisor holds many targets, each with a deal stage: screening, IC, passed, invested. Roles: owner, analyst, viewer.

Built for the keyboard

Jump to a target, a section or a source from the command palette. Open the next item that needs you with one shortcut.

07 · Models

Your models, connected on your provider's screen.

The platform never resells a model. You connect your own provider account by approving access on its own screen. The usage lands in your account, and the run is priced from your provider’s list.

  • Writers on the provider you pick. The method runs unchanged on the provider you connected.
  • A verifier from another family. It checks the writers with no tools, cheaply, against a different set of blind spots.
  • A pasted key is the last resort, and it is encrypted. Agents hold a run token. A broker uses your authorised connection and enforces your cap.
