MethodDraft

Why every claim gets a grade.

A memo is a pile of claims. Some come from a filing, some from a call, some from a model that sounded sure. If they all look the same on the page, the committee cannot tell which ones to push on.

We grade each claim A, B or C, tie it to a source id, and let the memo say how much of a section rests on each. It takes a minute to read, and it changes the meeting.

See it on a memo
AIn a filing, a contract or a data export
BReported by a credible source
CAn estimate, and flagged as one
