Evidence rules

No source, no claim.

Three rules make a memo defensible. They are written into the method, and the platform enforces them in code.

  • Every claim cites a source id and carries a grade. A claim with no source is rejected, unless it is graded C and says so.
  • What cannot be checked goes to the appendix, as data still needed, not into the argument.
  • Confidence is computed, not asserted. A section’s confidence comes from the mix of grades behind it.

Grades

A
VerifiedIn a primary source: a filing, a contract, a data export.
B
ReportedBy a credible source: a call, a trade report.
C
EstimatedFlagged as an estimate, and never presented as fact.

Source ids

S- web and public
P- provided by the target
I- interviews

Confidence

(A + ½B) / (A + B + C)
High 0.70 and above
Medium 0.45 and above
