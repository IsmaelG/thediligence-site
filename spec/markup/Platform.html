Questions

Before you sign in.

Do I have to paste an API key?

Not where your provider lets you sign in on its own screen. Where it does not, you can paste a key as a last resort. It is encrypted, never visible to agents, and you can delete it in one click. Ask for a connector.

What will a diligence cost me?

You see it before it runs. The plan lists each agent with its model and an estimate, you can switch to a cheaper or a stronger setting and watch the total move, and you set a cap. The run stops at the cap, and the actual cost sits next to the estimate.

Who can read my documents?

Your workspace members, and nobody else. Agents work in isolated sandboxes that hold no keys and can only reach a short list of hosts. Documents that a target shared with you are only sent to model providers that do not retain or train on them. The security page has the detail.

Can I take my work with me?

Yes. Memos export as Word and PDF, the ledger exports as a file, and your evidence goes with it. The method itself stays free, so you can run it without us.

Is this investment advice?

No. It produces evidence-graded analysis for the people who make the decision. You and your committee own the call, and every claim shows its source so you can check it.
