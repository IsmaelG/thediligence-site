Investor-operator · London · Commercial, P&L and turnarounds

Mazen Wazni

Builds, fixes and scales commercial businesses

Mazen Wazni builds, fixes and scales commercial businesses, with more than 20 years running P&Ls and teams in Europe and Africa. Most recently he was General Manager of Amazon Business Retail Europe, a multi-billion B2B ecommerce platform serving businesses from SMBs to large enterprises. Over 12 years at Amazon he also ran European Consumer Electronics P&Ls, managing category and supplier economics and turning around underperforming categories, and led 2,000 people in one of Amazon's largest UK fulfilment centres.

Before Amazon he built and ran consumer goods businesses across Africa, growing one from zero to $100M with 800 people. An engineer by training, he holds an IMD MBA.
